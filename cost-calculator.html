How costs and payback are calculated

Annual ice = plunges/week × 52 × bags/plunge × bag price.
Annual chiller running cost = daily kWh × 365 × electricity price + monthly upkeep × 12.

Totals scale the annual costs to your chosen months, adding the chiller setup cost once.

Simple payback in months = setup cost ÷ [(annual ice cost − annual chiller running cost) ÷ 12].

If chiller operation costs as much as or more than ice, there is no operating-cost payback. An eventual payback does not mean the chiller is cheaper within your chosen period.

Assumes year-round use and constant inputs. Excludes common tub and water costs, financing, resale, repairs beyond entered upkeep, and the value of your time. If the routes need differently priced tubs or other extra equipment, include that difference in the setup amount.
