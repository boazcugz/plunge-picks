How the ice calculation works

Heat lost by the water warms the ice to 0°C, melts it, then warms the meltwater to your target. Water is approximated as 1 kg per liter.

Ice kg = water liters × 4.186 × (start °C − target °C) ÷ [2.09 × (−ice °C) + 334 + 4.186 × target °C]

Constants: water heat capacity 4.186 and ice heat capacity 2.09 kJ/kg·°C; heat of fusion 334 kJ/kg. Sources: OpenStax on heat capacity and melting and latent heat.

The model excludes heat from your body, container, air and sun. It does not calculate cooling time, cold retention or a suitable exposure temperature. It does not apply directly to sealed ice packs or frozen bottles.
